Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) Explained

PPO is an "Actor-Critic" algorithm. Think of it as two brains working together to control your SC2 Marine:

  • The Actor (Policy \(\pi\)): Decides which way to move (North, South, East, West).
  • The Critic (Value Function \(V\)): Predicts how good the current spot is (The "Score" of the position).

Symbol Reference Guide

SymbolNameProject Usage
\(\pi\)PiPolicy: The Actor (Probability of actions).
\(V(s)\)ValueState Value: The Critic's score for a spot.
\(A_t\)AdvantageHow much better an action was than average (\(Q - V\)).
\(\delta_t\)DeltaTD-Error: The "surprise" after taking a step.
\(\gamma\)GammaDiscount Factor: How far we care about (0.99).
\(\lambda\)LambdaGAE Factor: Smoothing factor for Advantage.
Concept 1

The Critic: V(s) vs Q(s, a)

To understand Advantage, we first need to distinguish between the State Value and the Action Value.

V(s) - The State Value: "How good is this spot?" Average expected success from here.
Q(s, a) - The Action Value: "How good is this specific move?" Expected success if you move now.
\[A(s, a) = Q(s, a) - V(s)\] \[\text{Advantage} = \text{Specific Action Quality} - \text{Average Quality of the Spot}\]
Concept 2

Temporal Difference (TD) Error (\(\delta_t\))

This is the "Reality Check." It's the difference between what the Critic expected and what actually happened after one step.

\[\delta_t = \underbrace{r_t}_{\text{Reward received}} + \underbrace{\gamma V(s_{t+1})}_{\text{New predicted value}} - \underbrace{V(s_t)}_{\text{Old predicted value}}\]
Concept 3

The Advantage (\(A_t\))

In PPO, we use GAE (Generalized Advantage Estimation), which is a smoothed version of TD Errors:

\[\hat{A}_t = \delta_t + (\gamma\lambda)\delta_{t+1} + (\gamma\lambda)^2\delta_{t+2} + \dots\]

The "GPS Signal" Analogy (Bellman Equation)

\[V(s) = r + \gamma V(s')\]

Imagine your Marine is lost. The Beacon is a Radio Tower broadcasting a signal (20.0). Every step away, the signal drops by \(\gamma\) (0.99). The Marine simply follows the increasing signal strength (Advantage).

Pro Tip

The Edge Struggle: Why agents hate boundaries

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) "see" the map through kernels. At the Edge, the kernel has fewer neighbors to learn from (padding). This makes edges "lower resolution" than the center, often causing the Marine to stall or circle near map boundaries.

The Time-Travel Trick: The PPO Ratio

The Ratio measures how much more likely the move is now compared to the old brain snapshot:

\[\text{ratio} = \frac{\pi_{new}(a|s)}{\pi_{old}(a|s)}\]

If the ratio > 1.2, Clipping kicks in to prevent the brain from changing too fast. This keeps training stable.
